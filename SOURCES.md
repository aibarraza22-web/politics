- **2026-07-07 (live verification, this session):** Network policy opened for eia.gov + caiso.com + open-meteo/NASA (NREL hosts still blocked). Findings while working the checklist:
  - **eia930:** v2 API verified; its hourly `period` label is the hour ENDING (cross-checked against the bulk file's explicit "UTC Time at End of Hour"). Bulk CSVs adopted as primary for the imputation flags. Schema drift found: 2024 Jul_Dec switches to a 65-column layout splitting solar into "with/without Integrated Battery Storage", including EIA's own header typo "Solar witho Integrated Battery Storage (Adjusted)" — parser matches by regex and sums the split. Data-quality rule added: hours with solar > 1.25× BA fleet AC nameplate (e.g. AZPS 2024-01-25 = 3,822 MW vs a 919 MW fleet, unflagged by EIA) are folded into the unusable-hour flag (17 AZPS hours over 2022–24).
  - **eia860 (2024):** columns match parser; solar sheet includes CSP (Solana!) → Technology='Solar Photovoltaic' filter. Big structural finding: ~1.8 GW of AZ utility PV is interconnected to **CISO** (Agua Caliente, McFarland, …) and ~160 MW to WALC — outside AZPS/SRP/TEPC 930 series; modeling fleet = 89 plants / ~3.09 GW AC across the three AZ BAs. Phased build-outs (Eleven Mile, Sonoran) handled by per-generator in-service capacity fractions.
  - **eia923:** primeMover='ALL' rollup rows would double-count — filter primeMover='PV' (also excludes CSP from the calibration target).
  - **oasis:** see table row. AZ ELAP price history starts ~2023-04-01, so Estimator B covers Apr-2023+ only.
  - **weather:** NSRDB still unreachable (allowlist). Wired Open-Meteo ERA5-blend as documented stand-in; known biases absorbed by per-plant calibration + residual bands. **Action: add developer.nrel.gov to the environment allowlist, re-pull with `fetch_nsrdb_psm3`, and report the sensitivity of Estimator A to the weather source.**
