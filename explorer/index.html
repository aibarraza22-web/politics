Arizona Solar Waste Monitor

Arizona's utilities don't publish how much solar they curtail. This page estimates it from public data — physics-based potential vs. reported output, triangulated against CAISO's real-time prices at Arizona's grid nodes — with the uncertainty shown, never hidden.
